Sealed by Design

Sovereignty you can verify.

Four structural guarantees make NSE‑v5 not just powerful, but accountable — the way a state is accountable, not a black box.

Ed25519 Identity

Every node and agent carries a machine‑verifiable identity (L15). No impersonation, no ambiguous actor — sign, verify, and attribute to the exact key.

Layer 15 · Ethos & Identity

Multisig Quorum

High‑consequence operations only move when a threshold of independent signers agrees (L17). Power is deliberately distributed — no single point of control.

Layer 17 · Legal Sovereignty

Jailbroken Sandbox

Execution (L06) runs Restricted Python with no builtins, no shell, no filesystem escape — commands only inside a read‑only, caps‑dropped ephemeral container.

Layer 6 · Execution Sandbox

Machine Proof

Verification (L08) emits machine‑readable proof for critical invariants. Sovereignty stops being a slogan and becomes a checkable artifact.
